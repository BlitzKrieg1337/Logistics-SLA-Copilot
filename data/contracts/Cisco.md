# Master Service Agreement: Cisco Systems

**Effective Date:** June 10, 2025
**Vendor ID:** 1
**Region:** United States (San Jose, California)

## 1. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Cisco Systems guarantees delivery of networking equipment by the specified Expected Delivery Date.

## 2. Delivery Delays & Financial Penalties
In the event of a delay, Cisco Systems agrees to a penalty of **$300 USD per day**. 
Cisco allows a **1-day grace period** before penalties begin accruing.

## 3. Force Majeure
Standard exemptions apply for unforeseen global shipping disruptions.

## 4. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure
Both parties agree to maintain the strict confidentiality of all proprietary information, trade secrets, and operational data shared during the term of this Agreement. "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, pricing matrices, technical blueprints, API keys, customer lists, and logistics routing algorithms. This obligation survives the termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

## 5. Intellectual Property Rights
No transfer of intellectual property is implied or granted under this MSA. The Buyer retains all rights to its internal software, tracking dashboards, and brand assets. The Vendor retains all rights to its manufacturing processes, hardware designs, and internal ERP systems. Any custom integration code written collaboratively will be treated as joint intellectual property.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of profits, data, or business opportunities. The Vendor’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Buyer to the Vendor in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Audit and Compliance Rights
The Buyer reserves the right to conduct annual audits of the Vendor's supply chain practices, including environmental compliance, labor standards, and data security protocols (e.g., SOC 2 Type II compliance). The Vendor must provide at least 30 days' written notice before migrating data to a new geographic region.

## 8. Termination Clause
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing ninety (90) days written notice. In the event of a material breach—including chronic failure to meet the SLAs defined in Section 1 and 2—the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice if the breach remains uncured.
